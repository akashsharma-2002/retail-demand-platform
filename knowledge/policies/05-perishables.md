# Perishables Policy

## Maximum cover
Perishable FOODS items may not hold more than 10 days of forecast demand after delivery. For FOODS_3 fresh lines the
limit is 6 days. The limit overrides the service level target.

## Waste
Waste above 3% of weekly sales value in a department triggers a review of case packs and ordering days.

## Date rotation
Stores rotate stock first-expiry-first-out. Items within two days of expiry are marked down by 30%.
