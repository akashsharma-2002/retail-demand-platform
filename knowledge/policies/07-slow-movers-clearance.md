# Slow Movers and Clearance

## Definition
An item is a slow mover when it sells fewer than 2 units per week on average over the last 8 weeks.

## Ordering rules
Slow movers are ordered only when position falls to zero or below the MOQ. The platform uses the intermittent-demand
baseline for them in reviews.

## Clearance
Stock older than 90 days in HOBBIES or 60 days in HOUSEHOLD is marked down 25%, then 50% after another 30 days.
Cleared items are not reordered for 8 weeks.
