# Purchasing Budget Policy

## Weekly budget
Each store has a weekly purchasing budget equal to forecast cost of goods for the coming week plus 15% headroom.
Finance refreshes budgets every Sunday night from the approved forecast.

## When the budget binds
If the order plan exceeds the budget, the optimiser keeps the lines with the highest shortage cost per dollar
and trims the rest. Items with a service level of 95% are trimmed last.

## Over-budget requests
A plan above budget by up to 10% can be approved by the store approver. Above 10% needs the regional finance
partner. No plan may exceed 125% of budget.

## Month-end
In the last week of a fiscal month, headroom drops from 15% to 5% unless a seasonal event falls in the next 14 days.
