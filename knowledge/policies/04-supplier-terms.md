# Supplier Terms

## Lead times by department
Standard supplier lead times in days: FOODS_1 3, FOODS_2 5, FOODS_3 2, HOUSEHOLD_1 7, HOUSEHOLD_2 9,
HOBBIES_1 10, HOBBIES_2 14. Lead time is counted from order confirmation to receipt at the store.

## Minimum order quantities and case packs
Suppliers ship in whole case packs. FOODS case packs are 6, 12 or 24 units, HOUSEHOLD 2, 4 or 6, HOBBIES 1, 2 or 4.
Each item has a minimum order quantity of one or two case packs. Orders below MOQ are rejected by the supplier portal.

## Late deliveries
A delivery more than 2 days late counts as a supplier service failure. Three failures in a quarter trigger a supplier
review, and planners may add one day to that supplier's lead time until the review closes.

## Allocation
During shortages suppliers may allocate stock. Allocated lines are flagged SUPPLIER_ALLOCATION and are not counted
as planner overrides.
