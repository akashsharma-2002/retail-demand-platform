# Seasonal and Event Planning

## Calendar events
Sporting, cultural, national and religious events change demand. The forecast model uses the event calendar, but
planners should check large events manually: Super Bowl, Thanksgiving, Christmas and Easter.

## Christmas closure
Stores are closed on 25 December. No deliveries are scheduled on 24 or 26 December; orders due those days move to
23 December.

## SNAP days
SNAP benefit days raise FOODS demand in California stores, typically by 8-15% for FOODS_3. The model includes SNAP
flags, so planners should not add a manual uplift.

## Pre-event build
For Thanksgiving and Christmas, HOUSEHOLD and FOODS orders may be placed one week earlier than normal, with a budget
headroom of 25% for those two weeks.
