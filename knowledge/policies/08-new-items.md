# New Item Launch

## Initial order
New items with no sales history start with an initial order equal to 3 weeks of the category-average weekly sales for
comparable items, rounded to case packs.

## First weeks
For the first 6 weeks the forecast for a new item blends comparable-item demand with its own sales. Planners review
new items weekly and can override without a reason code.

## Delisting
An item that sells less than 30% of its comparable-item forecast for 8 weeks is proposed for delisting.
