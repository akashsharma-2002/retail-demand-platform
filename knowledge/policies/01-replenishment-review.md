# Replenishment Review Policy

## Review cadence
Every store reviews replenishment once a week, on Monday before 10:00 local time. The review period used in all
order calculations is 7 days. Orders placed after 14:00 are treated as next-day orders by suppliers.

## Order-up-to method
The order quantity is the stock needed to cover forecast demand over the supplier lead time plus the review period,
at the category service level, minus the current inventory position. Inventory position is on-hand stock plus
stock already on order. Quantities are rounded to whole case packs.

## Emergency orders
An emergency order outside the weekly review is allowed only when an item with more than $500 of weekly sales has
less than two days of cover. Emergency orders need approval from the store operations manager and are charged a
$25 expedite fee per supplier.

## Exceptions
Planners may override a system quantity by up to 20% without a reason code. Larger overrides need a reason code:
PROMO, LOCAL_EVENT, QUALITY_ISSUE or SUPPLIER_ALLOCATION.
